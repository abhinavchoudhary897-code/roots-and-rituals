03 / Listen

Stories in sound

Listen to a short retelling of a Warli folk story documented by IGNCA, or read it below.

The old man and the crab

This retelling was prepared with AI assistance from the IGNCA account. The read-aloud voice is generated by your browser, not recorded by a traditional storyteller.

A transcript is always available.

A recorded narration and waveform player have not yet been added.

Transcript

In a Warli painting described by IGNCA, a father with seven daughters struggles to carry wood home. A crab helps him. The youngest daughter agrees to marry the crab, despite her sisters' teasing. On entering his home, she discovers that her husband is a young man. This is a brief educational paraphrase of one documented version, not a claim about every Warli storytelling tradition.
